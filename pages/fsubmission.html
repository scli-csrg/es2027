ES 2027

 

Paper Submission

The 9th International Conference on Enterprise Systems (ES 2027) will be held at the University of the West of England (UWE Bristol), Bristol, England, on 13–14 June 2027. Prospective authors are invited to submit technical papers reporting original, previously unpublished work on the topics below.

Paper submission deadline: 25th Nov 2026  (see Important Dates for all deadlines)


Topics of Interest

Topics of interest include, but are not limited to:

Enterprise Systems Foundations & Architecture

  • Enterprise System Architecture and Integration
  • Business Process Management and Workflow Systems
  • Service-Oriented and Microservice Architectures for Enterprises
  • Cloud, Edge, and Hybrid Enterprise Infrastructures
  • Enterprise Systems in Regulated Industries (e.g., Finance, Healthcare)

Privacy

  • Privacy-Preserving Enterprise Data Processing
  • Differential Privacy and Secure Multi-Party Computation in Enterprise Systems
  • Privacy Risk Assessment and Compliance Automation (e.g., GDPR, PIPL)
  • PII Detection, Redaction, and Lifecycle Governance in Software Pipelines
  • Privacy-Aware Access Control and Auditability

Information Integration

  • Enterprise Data Integration and Interoperability
  • Semantic Integration and Knowledge Graphs for Enterprise Systems
  • Real-Time Data Pipelines and Event-Driven Integration
  • Integration of Heterogeneous Enterprise Applications and Legacy Systems
  • Data Quality, Provenance, and Trust in Integrated Enterprise Data

AI Safety & Trustworthiness

  • Safety Assurance of AI-Enabled Enterprise Systems
  • Security and Privacy Controls for Model Training, Deployment, and Inference
  • Adversarial Attacks, Defenses, and Red-Teaming in Enterprise AI
  • Hallucination Mitigation, Provenance, and Evidence-Based Guarantees for LLM Systems
  • Secure Model Supply Chain and Integrity for AI Artifacts
  • Human Oversight, Fail-Safe Mechanisms, and Accountability in Enterprise AI
  • Safety and Security of LLM-Based Agents and Multi-Agent Enterprise Workflows

AI-Driven Enterprise Systems (Cross-Disciplinary)

  • LLMs and Agentic AI for Enterprise Automation
  • AI for Software Engineering in Enterprise Contexts
  • Trustworthy AI Transformation in Enterprise Software Lifecycles

Submission Guidelines

  • Originality: only original papers that have not been published or submitted for publication elsewhere will be considered.
  • Format: all papers must be written in English using the IEEE conference template (double column, 10-point font).
  • Paper types and length: full papers presenting final results, up to 8 pages; short papers containing preliminary results, up to 6 pages; work-in-progress papers on ongoing projects, up to 4 pages.
  • Review: submissions follow a double-blind review policy and are assessed on technical quality, relevance, originality, significance, and clarity.
